About Lost In Pages

Created by two passionate book lovers, Lost In Pages exists to bring an unforgettable fantasy-inspired book event to the South Coast. Our goal is to create a welcoming space where readers, authors, narrators, artists and independent businesses can celebrate stories together.

More than a book signing

Expect bestselling and indie authors, talented narrators, unique vendors, bookish artwork, immersive experiences, Q&A sessions, exclusive merchandise and plenty of opportunities to meet fellow readers.

Built on transparency, creativity and community, every decision we make is centred around creating an event we'd love to attend ourselves.
